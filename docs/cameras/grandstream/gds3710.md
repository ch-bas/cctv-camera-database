## Features

- IP video door system with built-in hemispheric camera (180-degree wall-to-wall coverage)
- Day & Night white LEDs with smart brightness control (0.5 lux minimum illumination)
- Built-in RFID card reader (125KHz EM4100) for keyless entry, up to ~2000 cards
- SIP/VoIP 2-way audio and video streaming to SIP endpoints and GDS management software
- Built-in microphone and speaker for intercom
- Motion detection with up to 4 privacy masks
- 2 relay outputs for electric lock / light switch, 2 alarm inputs
- ONVIF Profile S compliant; manageable via GSURF Pro / GDS Manager
- Metal weatherproof and vandal-resistant casing (IP66 / IK09)
