- Frigate live view on iPhone/iPad (MSE) needs H.264 video and AAC audio. On Frigate 0.18.2 the reporter added a go2rtc restream of the main stream, ffmpeg:rtsp://127.0.0.1:8554/tapo_c250#video=h264#audio=aac, and set it as the camera live stream.
