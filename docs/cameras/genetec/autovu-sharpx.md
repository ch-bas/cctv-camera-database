## Features

- dedicated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) camera
- IP-based ALPR camera for mobile and fixed deployments; plate reads are processed by a separate AutoVu ALPR Processing Unit (2 or 4 camera ports, typically installed in the vehicle trunk)
- dual imagers: 1024x946 (XGA) monochrome progressive-scan ALPR camera + 640x480 color context camera, both 30 fps
- integrated pulsed LED illuminator that works in total darkness (0 lux)
- captures plates on vehicles travelling at speeds over 355 km/h (220 mph); reads across three lanes in mobile applications
- up to 4 ALPR cameras on the same processing unit
- identifies vehicle make and plate state/country of origin; speed and direction of travel with a single camera
- streams live video to Genetec Security Center
