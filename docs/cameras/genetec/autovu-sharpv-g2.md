## Features

- dedicated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) camera
- first/second-generation SharpV fixed ALPR unit with onboard ALPR processing
- dual imagers (LC models): 1280x960 monochrome progressive-scan ALPR sensor + 1280x960 color context sensor, 30 fps (60 Hz) or 25 fps (50 Hz); ITS (L) models are ALPR-only without context camera
- capture range: Standard (S) 2.75-18.25 m (9-60 ft), Long range (L) 18.25-35 m (60-115 ft)
- integrated pulsed LED illuminator usable in 0 lux; multiple wavelengths available
- manual zoom and focus adjustment
- onboard analytics: single-camera speed estimation, direction of travel, virtual loop, state name (North America and Europe)
- MJPEG or H.264 for ALPR and context video; JPEG stills
- 2 opto-isolated inputs (0-30 VDC) / 2 opto-isolated dry outputs (30 V / 100 mA)
- operating temperature -40 to 55 C for installations in India
- security white or black; pole and wall mount included
