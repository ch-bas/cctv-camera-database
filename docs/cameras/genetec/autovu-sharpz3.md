## Features

- dedicated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) camera
- mobile (vehicle-mounted) ALPR system; camera heads connect to a SharpZ3 base unit that performs onboard edge processing (Intel Atom E3950 per 2 ALPR ports + Intel Movidius Myriad X machine-learning co-processors)
- three optical sensors per camera: two 1456x1088 monochrome global-shutter ALPR sensors + one 1456x1088 color global-shutter context sensor (B&W night mode with 940 nm illuminator), 30 fps
- ALPR capture range up to 19 m (63 ft) with retro-reflective plates (8 mm lens 2.6-6 m, 12 mm 3.9-9 m, 16 mm 5.2-12 m, 25 mm 8.1-19 m)
- integrated pulsed LED illuminator usable in total darkness; 740, 850, 940 or 590 nm
- powered by AutoVu MLC machine-learning engine; built-in vehicle type, color and plate origin recognition; detects embossed and digital plates
- up to 4 ALPR cameras on the same base unit
- JPEG still images and MJPEG video streaming
- base unit: 2x Gigabit Ethernet, 4 digital inputs, 4 relay outputs; optional PoE+ and GNSS dead-reckoning navigation modules
- black version with sunshield 63 x 130 x 114 mm, 1.10 kg; available in black or white
- unified with Genetec Security Center
