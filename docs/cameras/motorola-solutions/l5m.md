## Features

- dedicated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) camera
- mobile (vehicle-mounted patrol) LPR camera with dual imagers: monochrome global-shutter IR LPR imager (1440x1080) + color global-shutter context imager
- captures plates on vehicles up to 180 mph (289 km/h) per 2026 datasheet (150 mph on 2021 EN datasheet)
- capture range by lens: 6mm 4-20 ft, 8mm 6-27 ft, 12mm 20-35 ft, 16mm 25-55 ft, 25mm 55-85 ft
- pulsed LED illumination 850nm and 730nm options, zero-lux operation, 8-85 ft
- continuous plate scanning up to 60 fps; up to four cameras per VIP edge appliance / VLP processor (LTE + GPS)
- vehicle make and model recognition
- ONVIF-compliant H.264/H.265/MJPEG video streaming (2021 EN datasheet)
- power 12.5W nominal, 25.5W max
- magnet, lightbar and vehicle-specific roof/trunk mounts
