## Features

- dedicated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) camera
- fixed LPR camera system with dual dedicated imagers: monochrome global-shutter IR LPR imager (1440x1080) + color global-shutter context imager (datasheet lists 1400x1800)
- captures plates on vehicles up to 150 mph (241 km/h)
- capture range by lens: 8mm 6-27 ft, 16mm 25-55 ft, 25mm 55-85 ft, 35mm 85-105 ft, 50mm 105-125 ft; up to 2 lanes in most scenarios
- pulsed LED illumination 850nm or 730nm options for zero-lux operation
- each sensor scans at 30 fps; up to 60 fps per camera with up to four cameras on one VIP edge appliance / VLP processor
- vehicle make, model and color recognition
- ONVIF-compliant video streaming (per product page)
- IP68 / NEMA 4 housing, shatterproof window, 3-axis precision aim lock
- 10-pin circular connector, Cat6 STP up to 100m
