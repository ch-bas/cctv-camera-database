## Features

- dedicated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) camera
- quick-deploy LPR camera with swappable internal battery, solar or AC/DC direct-line power
- captures plates up to 100 mph (161 km/h)
- capture range 16mm lens 30-75 ft (optimal 55 ft), 25mm lens 50-100 ft (optimal 75 ft); 24 ft (7.3 m) field of view, multi-lane coverage
- pulsed LED IR illumination: 850nm (covert) with 16mm, 730nm with 25mm
- onboard radar-based triggering by vehicle speed threshold and direction of travel
- collects plate, make, model, color and speed data
- internal LTE modem, WiFi 802.11a/b/g/n, Bluetooth 5 LE; configured via Mobile Companion app (Android/iOS)
- power consumption 9W max, 4W nominal
- tamper-proof design with paintable removable shroud
