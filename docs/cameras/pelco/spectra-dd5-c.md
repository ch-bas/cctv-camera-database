## Features

- original Spectra Series integrated dome system (camera, zoom lens, pan/tilt and receiver in one dome drive)
- 12X optical zoom, 8X electronic zoom; F1.6 f=4-48 mm; horizontal angle 47° wide to 4° tele
- high-resolution color camera, 460 TV lines, DSP III signal processing, S/N >46 dB
- 360° continuous pan rotation; unobstructed +2° to -92° tilt
- manual pan/tilt 0.5-40°/sec (150°/sec Turbo); preset speeds 250°/sec pan, 100°/sec tilt
- 64 presets with 20-character labels; 8 zones; 7 alarm inputs
- Auto Flip 180° at bottom of tilt travel; auto focus with manual override
- field-selectable multi-protocol receiver (Coaxitron and CM8500 D-series RS-485)
- in-ceiling and weatherized pendant back-box options; AC line-lock sync
